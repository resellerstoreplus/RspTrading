🛡️ What is RspTrading Escrow?

RspTrading Escrow Service acts as a trusted intermediary between the Buyer and Seller. Instead of sending payment directly to the Seller, the Buyer deposits the agreed amount with RspTrading. After the Account/Product is transferred and the Buyer confirms everything is correct, RspTrading releases the payment to the Seller.

01

Why Use Escrow?

🛡️

The Buyer does not need to send the payment directly to an unknown Seller.

🤝

🔍

Verification

02

How RspTrading Escrow Works

01

Buyer & Seller Agree

Both parties discuss the Account/Product, condition, price and all other important terms.

02

Seller → RspTrading

The Seller transfers the agreed Account/Product information or access to RspTrading for verification and transaction processing.

03

Buyer → RspTrading

The Buyer deposits the agreed transaction amount into the RspTrading Escrow.

04

Payment Verification

RspTrading verifies the payment before moving the transaction to the delivery stage.

05

RspTrading → Buyer

Once payment is confirmed, the Account/Product is delivered to the Buyer.

06

Buyer Confirmation

The Buyer checks the Account/Product and confirms that it has been received according to the agreed terms.

07

RspTrading → Seller

After the Buyer's confirmation, RspTrading releases the payment to the Seller.

04

Escrow Rules & Important Conditions

🤝

Agree Before Escrow

Buyer and Seller should agree on the Account/Product, price and conditions before starting the Escrow process.

🔐

Keep Information Secure

Do not publicly share passwords, recovery codes, 2FA codes or sensitive personal information.

🚫

No Direct Transaction

To use the protection of the RspTrading Escrow process, the transaction must actually go through the official RspTrading Escrow Service.

📋

Accurate Information

Both parties must provide accurate and complete information about the Account/Product and transaction.

05

Dispute & Suspicious Activity

⚠️ Transaction Review & Temporary Freeze

If RspTrading detects suspicious activity, inconsistent information, payment problems, an Account/Product issue, or unusual behavior during an Escrow Transaction, RspTrading may suspend the transaction and temporarily freeze the Escrow funds while the matter is reviewed.

Until the required verification is completed, RspTrading may delay releasing the funds or Account/Product to either party. The final action may depend on the available evidence, transaction records and applicable Escrow conditions.

⚠️
Important: RspTrading Escrow is designed to provide a safer transaction process, but no Escrow service can guarantee that every transaction will be completely risk-free. Always verify the Account/Product and agreed terms before proceeding.
🚫
Transactions outside RspTrading Escrow: If a Buyer or Seller chooses to bypass the official RspTrading Escrow Service and completes a direct transaction through another method, RspTrading will not be responsible for any resulting fraud, payment dispute, loss or other damage.
