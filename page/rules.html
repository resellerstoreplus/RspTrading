📜 Welcome to RspTrading Community

RspTrading Community is a community marketplace for Account Buy, Sell and Trade.

To keep the community safe, organized and trustworthy, all members are expected to follow the rules below.

01

Community Rules

🚫

No form of fraud, scam or intentionally providing false information is allowed.

❌

Fake screenshots, fake proof or misleading information must not be posted.

🤝

Buyers, sellers and all other members must be treated with respect.

📢

Only posts related to Account Buy, Sell and Trade are allowed.

🚫

Spam, unnecessary promotion or unrelated links are not allowed.

🛡️

Never publicly share passwords, recovery codes, 2FA codes or other important private information.

02

RspTrading Transaction & Escrow Rules

🛡️ Important Instructions for Buyers & Sellers

Verify everything before completing a trade.

Before buying, selling or trading any Account or Digital Product, Buyer and Seller should discuss the details carefully and verify the Account/Product information, condition, features and price.

After both parties agree on the price and other conditions, it is recommended to complete the Transaction through the Official RspTrading Escrow Service.

⚠️
Important: If any Buyer or Seller chooses to complete a Transaction directly through another method without using the RspTrading Escrow Service, and later suffers from fraud, payment problems or any other loss, RspTrading will not be responsible for that Transaction.
03

RspTrading Escrow Service Rules

01

Seller → RspTrading

The Seller transfers the Account/Product to RspTrading for the escrow transaction.

02

Buyer → RspTrading

The Buyer deposits the agreed full amount into RspTrading Escrow.

03

Payment Confirmation

After RspTrading receives and verifies the payment, the next stage of the Transaction will be confirmed.

04

RspTrading → Buyer

After payment confirmation, RspTrading transfers the Account/Product to the Buyer.

05

Buyer Confirmation

The Buyer confirms that the Account/Product has been received correctly and that everything meets the agreed conditions.

06

RspTrading → Seller

After receiving the Buyer's confirmation, RspTrading releases the payment to the Seller.

✅ This is how an RspTrading Escrow Transaction is completed.

04

Dispute / Suspicious Activity

⚠️ Dispute / Suspicious Activity

During an Escrow Transaction, if RspTrading notices any suspicious information, Account/Product issue, payment problem or unusual behavior from either Buyer or Seller, RspTrading reserves the right to suspend the Transaction and temporarily freeze the Escrow funds when necessary.

In such cases, until the necessary verification is completed, the money or Account/Product may not be released to either party.

05

Administration & Reporting

👮

Admins may remove suspicious posts, Accounts or members whenever necessary to protect the community.

💬

If you notice a problem, fraud allegation or suspicious activity, please report it to the Admin Team.

Stay Safe. Build Trust.

You can use the RspTrading Escrow process to complete your purchases and sales in a safer and more organized way. Stay safe and help build a trusted trading community.
